03. The Engineering Fieldbook

Open the interpretation ↗

The strongest technical narrative. Sage paper, blue drafting ink, margin annotations, and experience written as evidence.

Explore the conceptual diagram’s layers and expand implementation notes without leaving the page.

My recommendation.

Keep the original site’s dark editorial identity. Borrow the Journal’s hierarchy and the Fieldbook’s annotations: put scope, ownership, and a concrete result beside each significant role. Use one restrained interactive plate as a signature.

Flourishes worth carrying forward

Running chapter labels; numbered plates that explain something; margin notes that surface engineering decisions; an intentional print layout; visible keyboard focus; motion that visitors can choose. These details make the editorial premise useful.
